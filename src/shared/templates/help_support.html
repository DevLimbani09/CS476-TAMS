Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a weekly timecard?

Open Timecards, create a timecard for the appropriate period, enter your hours, and submit it for manager review.

Go to Create Timecard

Where can I see the status of a leave request?

Open the Leave Dashboard or Leave History to review your request and approval status.

Go to Leave History

What should I do if I forgot my password?

Use the Forgot Password page to request a secure password reset link for your corporate email.
